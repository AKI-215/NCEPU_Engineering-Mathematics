## Intended Users

- Graduate students taking related NCEPU (Beijing) courses
- Students reviewing Engineering Mathematics A/B or Engineering Mathematics Fundamentals
- Learners reviewing matrix theory, numerical analysis, and optimization basics

## Topic Coverage

The materials mainly cover:

- Matrix theory: matrix norms, generalized inverses, full-rank factorization, Jordan canonical form, LU decomposition, inner product spaces
- Numerical analysis: iterative methods for linear/nonlinear equations, interpolation, numerical integration and differentiation, numerical ODE methods
- Optimization basics: linear programming, simplex method, duality, KKT conditions, steepest descent

## Suggested Workflow

1. Read `2025工程数学基础重点--仅供参考(1).pdf` to establish the review scope.
2. Organize formulas, theorems, and common problem types by topic.
3. Practice with `19-24级工程数学A，B.pdf` under timed conditions.
4. Classify mistakes by topic and review the corresponding textbook or lecture notes.
